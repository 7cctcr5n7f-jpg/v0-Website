# TENROUNDS Google Business Profile Growth Playbook

Status: prepared for owner review. Nothing in this document has been published to Google.

## Objective

Turn the TENROUNDS Google Business Profile into a reliable local discovery and enquiry channel for Garsfontein and Pretoria East. The profile should accurately explain the 30-minute, coach-supported HIIT and boxing-fitness experience, help people find the studio, and make it easy to book a free first session.

## Operating rules

- Ask for honest reviews from genuine customers after a real interaction or visit. Never pay, reward, discount, gate or pressure anyone for a review.
- Never write a review on a customer's behalf or ask only customers expected to leave positive feedback.
- Obtain clear permission before publishing identifiable member or staff photos/videos. Respect anyone who declines; do not make participation a condition of membership.
- Do not show personal information, booking screens, medical details, payment details or member contact details in photos.
- Do not claim results, calorie burn, distances, opening hours, pricing or facilities unless verified and current.
- Reply to reviews professionally. Do not disclose membership or personal details in public replies.
- Keep business name, address, phone, opening hours and website consistent with the website's business profile data.

## Weekly content rhythm

| Day | Activity | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Capture one wide studio photo and one coach-led station photo | Fresh authentic image; confirm photo consent |
| Tuesday | Invite recent trial visitors and members to leave an honest review | Personal, non-incentivised review request |
| Wednesday | Publish a short workout or coach introduction update | One Google Business Profile post with a free-trial CTA |
| Thursday | Review new Google questions and reviews | Accurate replies and FAQ updates |
| Friday | Capture a short vertical video showing the ten-round format | One short video or photo update; no member shown without consent |
| Saturday | Check profile accuracy and answer new enquiries | Opening-hours, phone and booking-link check |

This is a quality-led cadence, not a requirement to publish every day. Skip a post if there is no useful, genuine update.

## Photo shot list

Capture real current images, not stock imagery:

1. Exterior entrance and signage, clearly showing where to enter.
2. Wide view of the training floor when tidy and safe.
3. Four dedicated boxing-bag stations.
4. Coach demonstrating safe bag technique.
5. Heart-rate display photographed without exposing member identities or personal data.
6. Functional strength stations and equipment.
7. A coach explaining the ten-round circuit.
8. Group atmosphere only when every identifiable person has consented.
9. Clean changing/amenity areas only if they are available to visitors and accurately represented.

Use well-lit, sharp, recent photos. Avoid heavy filters, misleading edits, excessive promotional text overlays and repetitive near-duplicates.

## Review request message

Send individually after a genuine visit or meaningful customer interaction. Do not send repeatedly to people who do not respond.

> Hi [First name], thanks for visiting TENROUNDS. If you have a moment, we'd appreciate an honest Google review about your experience. Your feedback helps local people understand what training with us is really like. No pressure at all: [Google review link]

## Review response principles

- Thank the reviewer naturally.
- Refer only to details they made public in their review.
- If feedback is negative, acknowledge the concern and invite them to contact the studio privately.
- Never argue, disclose personal details or offer a reward in exchange for changing a review.

## Monthly measurement

Record the Google Business Profile metrics for the same completed calendar month:

- Profile views, split between Search and Maps where available.
- Website clicks.
- Phone calls.
- Direction requests.
- Search terms, where the provider makes them available.
- New reviews, average rating and response coverage.
- Photos/videos published.

Compare like-for-like periods. Google Business Profile search-term reports can lag and may only be available for completed months. Do not interpret missing rows as zero.

## Website conversion link

Use a tagged link to the free-trial page once the owner approves the exact UTM convention, for example:

`https://tenrounds.co.za/free-trial?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=google_business_profile`

The site currently strips query strings from its own GA4 page-path reporting for privacy, so verify campaign attribution separately before relying on UTM performance.

## Owner approval checklist

Before anything is published, the owner should approve:
- The selected photo/video and documented consent.
- The post copy and CTA.
- Any change to profile details, categories, services or opening hours.
- Any response that could involve a customer complaint or sensitive matter.

No automated publishing or review solicitation is enabled by this playbook.
